# Civil Engineer A (Bryan/College Station) - review cycles and design coordination

## Summary

Civil engineer who handles site planning and permitting for projects in the Bryan/College Station area. Typical timeline: 2-3 months of site design plus 3-4 months of city permitting. The key pain is the back-and-forth with municipal review: even when no changes are needed, reviewers take about 2 weeks per round. Design coordination with architects, MEP and electrical is complex and improves with early involvement.

## Process

1. Acquire raw land (Houston has no zoning classification, but Bryan/College Station do).
2. Plat the property (preliminary plat, then final plat).
3. Design the site plan (2-3 months depending on project size).
4. City permitting (3-4 months typical).
5. Construction.

## City review cycle

- Standard review: 2 weeks.
- Address comments: 1-2 weeks.
- Resubmit and wait: 2 weeks, even if no scope change.
- Typically 2-3 rounds in total, even for unchanged submissions.

## Design coordination

- Site plan complexity increases as more parties join (architect, MEP, structural, electrical).
- Current method appears to be manual coordination.
- Early involvement of the civil engineer reduces rework.

## Opportunity angle

- He does not change plans between reviews but still waits 2 weeks each round.
- Design coordination is manual and fragmented.
- The permitting timeline is predictable but inflexible.
