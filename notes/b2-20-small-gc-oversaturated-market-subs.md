# Small GC Owner A (home and commercial) - oversaturated market, sub oversight

- Tools: estimates with notes on tablets, SketchUp Pro. Detailed drawing takes a while, so he only draws once payments are collected.
- Market: a "cesspool of hungry people bidding over and over"; oversaturated, "everyone is a contractor now," most just want to hang drywall and paint. Some scopes (HVAC, electrical) require real specialists.
- Subs: project managers are in place to watch subs. If a sub is not up to par they are cut; the contract allows cutting them without pay when a project runs long.
- Leads: organic, from Instagram or Google.
- Materials: cannot control acts of god (e.g., an order from another state delayed by a tornado).
- Growth: informal; relies on landing good jobs rather than a structured process.
