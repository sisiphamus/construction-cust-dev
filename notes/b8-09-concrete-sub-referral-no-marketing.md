# Concrete Sub Owner A (New York concrete subcontractor) - referral-driven, zero marketing, supplier quality is the only pain

## Observations from the interview

Impression: excellent customer service; sales will not be replaced.

- **Focus:** "We specialize in concrete. Everything we do is concrete. A jack of all trades is a master of none." Aims to do every project quickly and efficiently.
- **Background:** has run his own firm for about 30 years, started in concrete in the US after a few years of tough jobs abroad, bought his first van about five years in. Early on he met customers who never paid him and made many mistakes ("he learned the hard way"). Some people bullied him out of payment; now he is tough and, if he does not get the right vibe, will not give an estimate, let alone do the work.
- **Team:** 20-25 workers, with a long-time team leader (about 25 years) and four other team leaders. Longest-serving employee has been with him 29 years. He hires through his workers' own referrals. Pays top money and is flexible on days off and paperwork help. Hours roughly 7:00 am to 2:30 pm.
- **How he runs sales:** he meets the customer, looks at the job, prices it and signs off. Everyone on the team has his cell number; a young office assistant handles paperwork. He prefers customers call him directly: customers like dealing with the owner, whereas "when you deal with a big, big company, you never get to the owner."
- **Marketing:** zero marketing today. About 25-30% of work from referrals and about 80% from people seeing his crews at work and asking for his number. Some customers have used him for everything for 30 years. Early on he gave out business cards and advertised in community newspapers; "you get one job and another and another, like a tree."
- **Quality:** if there is a mistake they rip it out and redo it at no charge; "they never disappear."
- **Safety:** "number one thing is to avoid accidents; everyone gets home to family."
- **Seasonality:** May-October is busy. In New York, December-March concrete does not cure properly, so winter work shifts to demolition and excavation.
- **Technology:** accepts email, FaceTime and phones; "AI can never take the work I do." Does not expect robots to change his work. "The world will never be changed in construction, for the most part."
- **Pain:** over the past two years the concrete company he buys from has started skimping on costs and delivering worse concrete.
- **Philosophy:** "Always do the right thing. It doesn't cost anything to be kind." Loves his work, employees and customers.

## Follow-up feedback call (about six weeks later)

A product-feedback video call was scheduled, but only about two minutes of small talk were captured (his first time on this video platform; he usually uses WhatsApp/FaceTime). No substantive feedback captured.

## Relevance

Referral-only demand generation with a strong preference for personal, owner-to-customer contact: a counter-example to a marketing/lead-gen pitch. Low appetite for software or AI; material quality from suppliers is the only stated pain.
