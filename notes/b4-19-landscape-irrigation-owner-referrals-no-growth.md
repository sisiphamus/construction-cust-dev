# Landscape Sub Owner A (small landscaping and irrigation shop) - referrals only, labor shortage, no appetite to grow

- Work is mostly word of mouth, with the occasional commercial job that falls in his lap. One person in the office plus a few employees outside; he does all admin and invoicing himself and only does about 2 real hours of landscaping a day. No desire to grow: "what do you need a lot of money for?" Advice to the interviewer: if you really want to learn, work in landscaping.
- Software: QuickBooks ("if I can understand it, anybody can"), landscape design and irrigation design tools, plus Excel and Word.
- **Biggest problem:** hard to find workers; people no longer want physical work.
- **Lead sources:** a referral system where past clients refer new clients works well. He dislikes leads from Google or Yelp: they expect too much for too little work, and there is no trust (they look over your shoulder; "you can have all the ratings you want and still people won't trust you").
- **Pricing stance:** "You don't give free nothing." No free consultations: serious customers pay for the consult and the design, and he may give some things free later.
