# Landscape Subcontractor A (very small landscaping/irrigation business) - referrals, no growth

- Marketing is word of mouth; occasional commercial jobs when they fall in his lap. One person in the office plus a few field employees; he does all invoicing and admin himself and spends only about two real hours a day landscaping.
- Not interested in growing: "What do you need a lot of money for?"
- Tools: QuickBooks ("If I can understand it, anybody can"), landscape and irrigation design software, Excel and Word.
- Biggest problem: finding workers; people do not want physical work anymore.
- Referral system from past clients works well. Dislikes leads from Google or Yelp: those clients expect too much for too little work and are wary and untrusting ("you can have all the ratings you want and still people won't trust you"; "you don't want people looking over your shoulder").
- Pricing stance: "You don't give free nothing." No free consultations; serious customers pay for the consultation and the design up front, and he can credit it later.
- Lead-gen play: low interest in paid lead sources; trust-based referrals preferred.
