# Vendor Founder B (code-compliance AI startup) - deterministic, auditable compliance and an inspections angle

Interviewer 2 attended silently; Interviewer 1 led.

## Background
She co-founded the startup with a licensed-architect partner of ~20 years. Origin: she developed an arts-center adaptive-reuse project, ran out of money because of permitting delays, and set out to fix the problem. Interviewer 1 first reached out earlier and then met the team at a major architecture conference.

## Technical approach and market
- Two structured datasets (regulatory text and 3D design elements) compared deterministically, not probabilistic LLM review. Output is an auditable report of everything in AND out of compliance. A report of what passed matters in a risk-averse industry that needs assurance, not just a list of problems; she credits this for traction.
- Human QA on every module before release: "every error you present in this market is 10x worse than a data point you don't have." Slower to release than a competitor but higher accuracy standard.
- Competitor critique (Interviewer 1's research): UpCodes expands fast but relies partly on customer bug reports to keep code accurate; she agreed this is backwards and dangerous.

## Reviewer bias and timeline reliability
Interviewer 1 relayed a developer's anecdotes (a reviewer demanding a yellow building, a bus lane at a studio-apartment project). She validated the framing of "risk analysis around the interpretive layer": forecasting accurately given reviewer behavior has real financial value (Interviewer 1 cited ~$150k per month of construction-loan interest). Timeline reliability, not just speed, is what developers want (a "delivery guarantee" analogy).

## Inspections angle
She suggested a space nobody is pursuing: compare city-approved plans to as-built site conditions before an official inspector arrives so contractors can self-correct before failing inspection. Requires computer vision plus approved-plan ingestion; a live site-scanning company could partner.

## Other advice
File provisional patents on anything built and protect IP. She offered investor and collaboration conversations; omitted here. No buy signal (competitor/peer).
