# Vendor BD Director A (construction-data SaaS vendor) - materials intelligence platform demo

## Summary

A discussion at an architecture conference where a rep demonstrated the vendor's 3.7M-project construction database and new Material Intelligence product. Building product manufacturers can search which projects use their products and where architects specify them. The platform scrapes public construction data (via public-records requests and web bots), updates private data through a content team of ~500 (about 1,200 total with offshore) who call contractors and developers daily, and uses OCR plus AI to extract product-level detail from plans and specs. Manufacturers pay to be listed as "basis of design" rather than "or equal" so architects default to their products.

## Key points

- **Ownership/structure.** Owned by a billion-dollar industrial conglomerate; a sister company sells design software to architects/engineers (~65,000 architects) and is the data product's biggest customer, embedding the product-spec library.
- **Coverage.** ~3.7M active projects: civil (airports, dams, bridges, sewers, water/wastewater) and general (commercial, community, condos, multifamily), but NOT single-family housing. Rationale: single-family is easy to pull from permits but messy and low-value; commercial gives more stable, predictable revenue and lower default risk.
- **Content sourcing.** Public data plus a large manual content team phoning contractors and developers. Relationships with contractors and architects are the moat.
- **Material Intelligence.** OCR keyword search across plans/specs (a concrete-product brand query returns 55K+ projects). Manufacturers see which architects specify competitors and approach them for the next project. Basis-of-design strategy: be the required product, not "or equal."
- **Analytics.** Dashboard tracks construction trends by state/county/MSA and multifamily vs single-family spend.
- **Customers.** Large building-product manufacturers (insulation, drywall).
- **Sales org.** BDRs prospect, then sales reps demo, negotiate and close. One person handles collections, mostly net-30 to net-90 term conversations; small defaults not chased.
- **Competitor/peer.** A code-database company (UpCodes) also approaches them regularly about sales process and market engagement.
- **Relevance to interviewers.** Validates a queryable construction project database and material intelligence from specs.
