# Small GC Owner A - oversaturated market, sub management, organic leads

- **Tools:** estimates with notes on iPads, SketchUp Pro. Drawing takes a while, so he only draws once payment is collected.
- **Market:** oversaturated; "everyone is a contractor now." A cesspool of hungry people bidding over and over. Most guys just want to hang drywall and paint, while some scopes (HVAC, electrical, etc.) require much more.
- **Sub management:** project managers watch the subs. If someone is not up to par they are gone; sometimes the project runs long, but he cuts them and does not pay them, which is written into the contract.
- **Leads:** organic, mostly Instagram and Google.
- **Supply risk:** cannot control acts of god; for example, if he orders something from a region hit by a tornado, that is simply that.
- **Style:** "I probably don't do things the way they're supposed to be done... I pray." He grows by hoping for good jobs that are not corrupt.
