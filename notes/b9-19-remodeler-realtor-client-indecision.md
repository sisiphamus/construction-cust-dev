# Realtor/Remodeler A (small residential remodeling operation) - delays come from indecision and overbooking

- **Delays are mostly client indecision.** The more decisions made ahead of time, the more timely the job. Changes ripple and force the whole plan to be tweaked. Appliances, tile and similar have long lead times, so she runs a tight schedule and orders in advance.
- **Tools evolution:** paper, then Word, then shared notes, then tried Houzz ("expensive for what we used it for"; had lots of capability they did not use; its room-scan drawing for bathroom remodels was not accurate, so she uses SketchUp instead); accounting is outsourced. Now uses Google Calendar with a separate calendar for every project.
- **Team:** same subs for many years (about 21), changing from time to time; asks subs for recommendations for new subs. She works with 5-10 builders and pulls from one of them. Notes heavy alcohol and drug use in the trades.
- **Materials:** no real weather issues; materials trouble only during COVID. "I try to source materials locally." Prices are high now.
- **Why jobs run late: overbooking.** "You can't charge a client enough to only run one client at a time." Summer is the busy season; mid-size and small operators are slow outside it, spend the first quarter bidding and local jobs, then overload themselves to make up the money, taking every job even though they know they cannot manage it. Need to keep crews busy, "but you should really not take the job sometimes."
