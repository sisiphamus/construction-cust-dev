# Landscape/Irrigation Owner A (small shop) - referral-only, labor shortage, no appetite to grow

- Work comes almost entirely by word of mouth; occasional commercial jobs "if it falls in their lap." One person in the office plus a few field employees; the owner does all admin (invoicing etc.) and spends only about two real hours a day landscaping.
- No desire to grow: "What do you need a lot of money for?"
- Software: QuickBooks accounting ("If I can understand it, anybody can"), landscape and irrigation design software, plus Excel and Word.
- Biggest problem: hard to find workers; "people don't want physical work anymore."
- Referral system from past clients works well. Dislikes leads from Google or Yelp: clients expect too much for too little work, and trust is low ("you can have all the ratings you want, and still people won't trust you"). Customers who look over your shoulder make the work unpleasant.
- Pricing policy: "You don't give free nothing." No free consultations; serious customers pay up front for the consult and design, after which some of it can be credited.
- Advice to the interviewer: go work in the landscaping industry if you really want to learn it.
