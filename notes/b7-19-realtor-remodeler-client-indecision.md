# Realtor-Remodeler A - residential remodels, client indecision, tools

## Delays and scheduling

- Residential delays are caused by indecision: the more decisions made ahead of time, the more timely the job. Changes ripple and cause the whole plan to be tweaked.
- Appliances, tile and similar items have long lead times, so she runs a tight schedule and orders in advance.
- No real weather problems. Material problems only during COVID. "I try to source materials locally." Prices are high now.

## Tools

- Over 20 years: paper, then Word, now shared notes visible to all sides. Tried Houzz but it was expensive for what they used; it had much more capability than they used (they outsource accounting). Houzz room scanning for bathroom remodel drawings was not accurate; SketchUp is used instead.
- Now runs Google Calendar with a separate calendar per project.

## Team

- Same sub team for many years (some about 21 years), with changes from time to time; she asks subs for recommendations for new subs. Her team works with 5-10 builders and she pulls from one of them as needed.
- Alcohol and drug use is a problem in the trades.

## Why projects run late (her view)

- You cannot charge a client enough to run only one client at a time, so small and mid-size contractors overbook.
- Summer is the busy season. Small and mid-size contractors are slow outside it, so they take every job they can even though they know they cannot manage them all. In the first quarter all they do is bid jobs and local work; to make up the money later they overwhelm themselves with jobs.
- "Need to keep the guys busy... but you should really not take the job sometimes."
