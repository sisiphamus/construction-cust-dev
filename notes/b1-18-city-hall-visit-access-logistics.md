# City Innovation Director A (large Texas city) - failed walk-in attempt

- Context: students doing permitting research, bringing donuts as an icebreaker, trying to see the innovation director on an upper floor.
- Reception and security require an appointment and a phone confirmation to go to any floor; no walk-up access. Offices they had called ahead were not answering. They had a student ID but not a driver's license.
- Plan: go to the planning floor, where walk-ins are accepted, and ask for help setting up a meeting on another floor (this led to the planning-department walk-in interview).
- Lesson: planning staff accept walk-ins; executive offices need scheduled appointments.
