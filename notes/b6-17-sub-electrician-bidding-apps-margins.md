# Electrical Sub Owner A (small Texas electrical contractor) - price, bidding apps, GC distrust

- Finds projects through bid-matching apps (PlanHub and Bluebook); employs an estimator. Work mix: service, commercial, and industrial (manufacturing, metals, factories). Pays electricians hourly; scales by keeping crews busy so he does not lose the team.
- Biggest issue: "THE PRICE." Wire prices climbed while GCs tried to hold old prices; subs had higher expenses. Apps let everyone bid and push competition "like crazy"; many electricians in Texas. To win he sets margin at ~10% (a $50k job nets ~$5k). Change orders are subs' favorite because the contract is locked and they are nearly all profit.
- GCs: "Anybody can call themselves a GC. No license... tomorrow you could call yourself a GC." Believes GCs are unregulated and get shady skimming costs; "worked a project for 3 months and only got a few grand" under a bad GC. Better compensated without GCs, especially residential; prefers to be just an electrical contractor. Tries to build relationships with GCs; apps show how often GCs award work.
- Payment structure example: $10k job paid as $5k first, $2.5k trim-out, $2.5k final. If paperwork is right, draws/financing are fine.
- Regional note: fewer electricians work in a rural border region, giving more lock on the market there.
- Marketing: to succeed you need a marketing fund; watch reviews and spend thousands per month on ads. Older contractors are established; new ones need the fund.
