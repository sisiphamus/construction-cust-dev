# GC Owner A (New Orleans commercial GC and permit expediter) - permits are a float, not a profit center

Interviewer 1 on a call. The GC takes ~99% of his time; the expediting firm started after he noticed no expediters existed in the city and now mainly generates leads for the GC. Expediting clients are mostly out-of-town retail chains and contractors (national retailers, mall tenants). He rarely gets GC clients because he competes with them.

## Key insights
- **Permits as reimbursable:** standard AIA templates classify permits as reimbursable with no markup, so contractors float $5,000-$50,000 for 60-90 days at zero markup and zero finance fee. He argues it should be a profit center with at least a markup to cover financing, like insurance premiums (every contractor marks up general liability). He has tried to change this convention locally with limited success because the template just says "permits reimbursable".
- **Expediter discovery problem:** people who look for expediters already know they exist; the average person starting a project does not think to find one (unlike finding a GC or architect).
- **Turnover at permit offices:** "you don't necessarily have a guy because the guy you got might be gone next week", limiting relationship-based expediting.
- **What expediters do:** tedious tracking, follow-up, written communication, occasional in-person trips to city hall. They cannot cure government ineptitude, only document and follow up.
- **Jurisdictional complexity:** city, state fire marshal (life safety and ADA), and historic preservation commissions often conflict. Example: in a historic building the fire marshal required an ADA lift while the historic board would not allow it at the front porch. Processes differ across parishes.
- **Online portals:** most major jurisdictions have adopted them, rural areas lag badly, and the paper-to-portal transition creates new confusion.
- **Business cycle:** contractors feel news about six months late: tariffs and interest-rate changes hit GCs when active projects wrap and the pipeline is empty. Ongoing challenges: real estate cycles, speculative development, material and labor costs.

## Product-relevant angles
Permit cost modeling and markup education; jurisdiction mapping and process differentiation; expediter discovery.
