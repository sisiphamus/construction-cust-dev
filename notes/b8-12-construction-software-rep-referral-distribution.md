# Software Vendor Rep A (Canadian construction SaaS) - referral-dependent distribution; cold call

## Summary

Interviewer 2 called a rep at a construction-software company to learn about their distribution challenge and pitch the interviewers' demo-booking service. The recording starts mid-conversation with the rep discussing how tradespeople resist learning new software ("they don't want to look at anything new"), which Interviewer 2 had heard before from people in the trades.

## What the rep described

- The product is good and already built; the job is getting it into as many companies' hands as possible.
- Growth is referral-based: one customer comes in, tells his friends, and others follow. Hard to break into new accounts without a warm introduction.
- Long contracts and long sales cycles, comparable to large incumbent platforms that lock customers into long contracts.
- Distribution is the ceiling.

## Pitch and pricing

Interviewer 2 said they book demos with prospects for companies and are paid per booked demo. Pricing is tied to deal size: for a consumer brand it might be a few dollars per booking; for larger enterprise clients (preferred) about 1-2% of deal value. Typical market rate is 3-5%; they charge less because they are young. Interviewer 2 said he was not looking for a job and had been calling partly out of curiosity after working with a similar Canadian construction-software company.

## Buy signal

Pay-if-fixed. The rep described the exact problem and received the 1-2%-of-deal-value framing without objection, but only agreed to receive information. The call ended informally with holiday well-wishes.
