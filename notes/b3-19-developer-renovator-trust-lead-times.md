# Developer D (developer-renovator, aging multifamily) - trust, lead times, change-order scams

## Takeaways
- **Lead times:** 6-10 months for a property to get an electric connection, so it must be ordered far in advance. Windows and doors take 4-8 weeks (the GC works with procurement). Regular materials have no huge delays; custom roofs or windows do.
- **Model:** developer and renovation company in one; renovation all in house. Always buys to renovate; currently has enough work in his own properties. Targets properties from the 1970s to 2000s, at least 20 years old; beyond about 50 years old they need too much maintenance. Started with single-family, now focuses on multifamily.
- **Sourcing deals:** relationships with major brokerages, who need to know you before they trust you (proof of funds, etc.). They send a huge list that is slowly narrowed, now that they know what he wants. He targets properties with poor management or in up-and-coming areas.
- **Trust is the theme:** his best rule in real estate is "don't trust anyone." Brokers only get paid when they sell. He had one wonderful GC, but "very rare"; usually "they try to scam you... you sign the agreement and then hit you with change orders over and over."
- **How he picks GCs:** word of mouth; constantly compares GCs against each other.
- **Buy signal:** curious; open to staying in touch and to the interviewers looking at his Florida properties.
