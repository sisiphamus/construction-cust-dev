# SaaS Rep A (code-compliance and permit-workflow vendor) - how to sell to jurisdictions

## About the vendor
- Code compliance plus permit workflow SaaS; 30+ California jurisdictions (including large and mid-size cities and Bay Area cities); ~10-person team. Also works with architects (said to spend ~70% of their time on admin and communication) and general contractors.
- Current focus is "workflows": zone analysis, document completeness check, QC workflows.
- Code database: licensed ICC codes, split by year and code group, with RAG search. Municipal amendment databases are built partly by automation/scraping and partly on customer request, not by calling every city hall.
- Does not integrate with legacy permit tools; runs as a fully web-based side tool, betting those older tools will die.

## Go-to-market lessons
- Selling to jurisdictions is "not as hard as you think" if you find people in real pain: they are severely understaffed and it takes about 5 years to train a plan reviewer.
- Entry tactic: land via a credit-card-limit budget to avoid full procurement approval, then upsell annually.
- Org structure matters: plan checkers do not buy; the senior building official does. Jurisdictions are friendly but you must understand who decides.
- Trust problem: Interviewer 2 pressed on code accuracy; the rep acknowledged AI outputs must be verifiable and has heard this repeatedly.

## Relevance
Competitor or adjacent product in code compliance; jurisdiction GTM tactics.
