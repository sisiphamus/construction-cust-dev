# SaaS Rep A (subcontractor pre-qualification vendor) - one form for all GCs

## Summary
Interviewer 2 reached out as a student entrepreneur in Houston (building compliance and permitting software and talking with local subs, GCs and developers). Houston contacts complain about pre-qualification and say bidding is very competitive in the market. The vendor rep covers Texas and was enthusiastic about Houston's growth.

## How the product works (per rep)
- The vendor is a third party hired by general contractors. Subcontractor data is collected once, run through a formula/algorithm approved by the participating GCs, and produces a risk-analysis score. GCs use the score to assess risk and assign aggregate (bonding/limit) amounts.
- Pre-qualification starts with GCs requesting it; the vendor then builds a network so subs get a "handshake" with GCs. Subs get feedback on how to improve their score (financials, health and safety criteria) and strong subs are put in front of GCs.
- Pain it targets: subs currently send a separate email/PDF form to each GC, and use several different tools (ISN, Builders Connected were mentioned as ones Houston subs dislike). Pitch: fill in one universal form and be pre-qualified with every GC on the network.
- Two demo tracks exist: GC-level and subcontractor-level. Rep said it is growing substantially in Texas.
