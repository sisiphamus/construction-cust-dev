# Vendor Rep B (field-service SaaS) - sales call pitching an all-in-one platform for trades

## Notes
- The rep says many customers hardly last past 1 year in business; when the interviewer said he had been at it 2.5 years, the rep was shocked because that is a long time.
- He speaks to many electricians: the number one thing he hears is that managing everything is difficult. Many still use pen and paper for invoices because they have always done so. "I'd argue [the product] is even more simple than spreadsheets."
- Process: a generalist first, then a product expert shows how others use it and tailors a plan. Subscription includes onboarding training over multiple sessions.
- Promise: the product either brings in more customers or increases job size; claimed average revenue lift of 37%.
- Pushed: a referral system (subs typically offer clients 25% off the next order for a referral), Google reviews ("really focus on this"), email campaigns, and reaching back out on missed jobs.

## Discovery questions the rep asked (a useful checklist of owner-operator pains)
Tracking win rate? Upselling beyond what the customer called about? Quote on the spot or later? Who handles follow-up? How are calls taken? Is there a shared calendar? Missing jobs or showing up late? How are hours managed (clock in/out)? How are credit card payments processed? Doing everything through spreadsheets is "crazy."

## What the platform covers (as pitched)
Central customer system, quoting, scheduling employees and jobs, customer communications, expense and labor cost tracking, material expenses, invoicing, collecting timely payment and avoiding large receivables, payment processing, after-sales process, marketing.

## Pain surfaced
Paying for accounting software plus other tools plus a manual time clock creates disorganization. The electrician's day: 8am to 8pm, then 2-3 more hours of admin.
