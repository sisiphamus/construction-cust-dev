# Event Space Operator A - build-out delayed by city electrical and plumbing approvals

- The original partner built the old venue. For the new space they used the contractor the landlord uses for all of its properties; the landlord paid for the build-out as part of getting into the lease. The design was sketched out and the crew asked to replicate it closely; some work was outsourced through the old partner and some builders came from the landlord.
- The space was already a building; they only had to transfer equipment. When they walked in it was fully exposed, with no electrical done.
- **Most frustrating part:** waiting for the city to approve electrical and plumbing, after which everything else could proceed. Meanwhile they were already scheduling events.
- "Timing is super important, it ruins trust."
