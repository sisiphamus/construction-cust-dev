# Building Owner A (repeat buyer of trades services) - trust and likeability beat price

Informal, non-industry conversation; short note.

- **Trust over price.** She keeps a tradesperson who charges $156 against competitors at $99 because "she likes him" and he is honest. Her view: at large companies the workers lack character; and a union member is likely good because they must be trained for the union.
- **Recommended vendor:** a large plumbing group she used mainly for steam-boiler maintenance. Very good plumbers, family-run, not "nice." Two incidents: (1) she contracted a new hot water boiler (a few thousand dollars) and the crew arrived with a large dent in the unit; she refused it; there was a big fight with the owner, who said never to call again, but he ultimately delivered a new undented boiler and installed it properly. (2) They once billed $200 more than agreed; when she said she had cameras in the building, the owner said the $200 was for a parking ticket, then admitted he might not have gotten one.
- Takeaway for customer development: for a repeat buyer, relationship, honesty and fair billing dominate; she tolerates a prickly vendor if quality is high.
