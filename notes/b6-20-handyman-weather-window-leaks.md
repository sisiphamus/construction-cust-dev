# Handyman Owner A (local handyman franchise) - weather and scheduling delays; leak diagnosis

- Pain: weather delays (cannot attach a window in rain), constant rescheduling, customers only free nights and weekends, appointments running long, and unreliable technician availability (an employee absent with no return date).
- "We do everything. You'd be surprised how much goes into that."
- Typical issue: window sealant failure. Condensation or sun exposure cracks the seal over years; moisture seeps in, rots sheetrock and framing, and eventually causes mold. How much sun a window gets drives failure; some customers choose to replace all windows for peace of mind.
- Diagnosis: find the source (window, stucco cracks, roof) with a water test: one person with a moisture meter inside and one outside with a pump. If it is not the window they refer to another trade.
- Double-pane windows with argon fail by fogging, meaning the glass unit needs replacement; failed glass-to-frame sealant lets the pane wiggle and water creep in; old windows can also fail from shifted framing.
