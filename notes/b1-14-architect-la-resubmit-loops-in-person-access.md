# Architect C (Los Angeles architecture firm) - the resubmit loop and lost in-person access

Three questions were asked by Interviewer 1; answers were written.

1. Most frequent delay struggle? "Not anticipating important issues that will come up as part of the process and not diligently resolving all comments received by the City or County before resubmitting plans." The time sink is the iteration phase (incomplete resubmittals trigger further rejections), not missing documents upfront.
2. Do relationships in the permit office speed things up? "It helps in the sense that you know what they are expecting and how to deliver on that." Calibration and trust, not favoritism.
3. What would you change with total control? "I would probably bring back the ability to meet people in-person to resolve issues. The move to online reviews Post-COVID has brought many benefits, but it's much harder to meet people in real time now to resolve issues. You used to be able to go to the City and meet all the different departments at the same time. It's impossible to do that now." Online review made feedback sequential and isolated by department, lengthening the loop.

## Takeaways
- Resubmit loops, not upfront paperwork, are the gating factor.
- Value of relationships is learning how reviewers want submissions packaged.
- Structural gap since the pandemic: no simultaneous multi-department access.
