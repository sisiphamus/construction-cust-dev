# Handyman A (small residential repair business) - weather and availability reschedules

## Pains
- Weather: if it is raining the window cannot be attached; lots of rescheduling. Customers work for a living so only nights and weekends are available; appointments run long; staff availability is unpredictable and he sometimes has no return date for a crew member.
- "We do everything. You'd be surprised how much goes into that."

## Technical detail on leaks
- Condensation and dew get into window nooks; heat then cracks the window seals over years. Moisture slowly rots drywall and framing and can lead to mold. Severity depends on how much sun a window gets; some customers replace all windows for peace of mind.
- Diagnosing water intrusion: determine whether water comes from the window, stucco cracks or roof. Water test: one person with a moisture meter inside, one outside with a pump. If it is not the window, a different trade is called.
- Double-pane windows with argon fill: when they fail the glass fogs and needs replacement. The sealant where glass meets frame fails and water creeps in. Old windows can also fail because framing has shifted.
