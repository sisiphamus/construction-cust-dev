# Developer-Renovator A - lead times, trust in brokers and GCs

## Lead times

- 6-10 months for a property to get an electric utility connection, so it must be ordered in advance.
- Windows and doors take 4-8 weeks; the GC works with procurement. Regular materials have no huge delays; custom roofs or windows do.

## Business model

- A developer and renovation company in one; renovation is done entirely in-house. They currently have enough work in their existing properties and always buy to renovate.
- Started with single-family and moved to multifamily. Properties from the 1970s to 2000s usually need renovation (at least 20 years old; at about 50 years there is too much maintenance). They target properties where they know management is poor or the area is on the rise.
- Deal sourcing runs through relationships with large national commercial brokerages: brokers must know you before they trust you (proof of funds, etc.). They send a huge list that is slowly narrowed down, by him or now by brokers who know what he wants.

## Trust theme

- "Best rule I know in real estate: don't trust anyone." Brokers only receive commission when they sell. On construction, he had one wonderful GC, but that is very rare; usually GCs try to scam you: you sign the agreement and then they hit you with change orders over and over.
- GC choice runs on word-of-mouth recommendations and constantly comparing GCs to each other.
