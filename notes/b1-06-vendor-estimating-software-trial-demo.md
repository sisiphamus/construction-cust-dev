# Vendor Rep A (home-remodeling / contractor SaaS) - AI zip-code estimates vs spreadsheets

Note: here the interviewer plays the prospective buyer, so this is a product-adoption view of a small contractor team (four users), not a typical discovery interview.

## Adoption and usability
- The team currently estimates in Google Sheets and Excel: manual and time-consuming.
- Interviewer said the software felt complicated for one team member compared with other tools evaluated. The rep's answer: training. Ongoing live training with US-based staff, a post-trial implementation call (account, logo, seats, company info), and a proposed group meeting with all four users to assess fit.

## AI-powered estimates
- Interest was strongest here. Estimates adjust by zip code with breakdowns of demolition, material and labor, with low / medium / high ranges; the middle option reflects competitive local rates. Example used: retiling a ceramic-tile floor, with area and tile type as inputs.
- Templates by trade (drywall, carpentry etc.). Pricing data comes from a paid third-party provider; exact method proprietary.
- Customizable line items, photos, and client sign-off to protect the contractor.
- Value stated: avoids researching prices across multiple websites.

## Fit and scale concerns
- Interviewer asked whether the product is built for large or ground-up construction firms rather than small contractors. Rep said it supports solo operators to companies with hundreds of staff, and pitched early adoption as a way to compete with bigger firms.
- Positioned as a full business system: projects, estimates, subcontractors, scheduling, invoicing, client payments and financing (financing valued as the business scales), plus marketing/listings and website management.

## Commercial
- 30-day free trial; decision deadline at expiry; follow-up on Friday; online discounts and incentives hinted for signing before the trial ends. Interviewer asked for a clear price figure and discount to justify ROI against alternatives.
- Call quality issues interrupted the remote demo several times.

## Signals
- Buy signal: curious but undecided; adoption friction (multi-user complexity, spreadsheet habit) is the main barrier. The one-click, location-adjusted estimate is the feature that resonates.
