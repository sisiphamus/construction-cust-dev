# Houston City Hall visit - failed walk-up to reach the city innovation director

Interviewers 1 and 2 were doing permitting research and brought donuts as an icebreaker. They tried to reach a City Innovation Director, said to manage changes at the permitting office, on an upper floor.

- Reception/security required an appointment and a host confirming by phone for any floor; no walk-up access. They had student ID but not a driver's license.
- Offices they had called ahead were not picking up the phone.
- Plan B was to go to a lower floor and ask someone to help schedule a meeting on another floor.
- No substantive permitting content. Lesson: access to city officials requires prior scheduling; cold walk-ins fail.
