# Developer C (large commercial developer, Florida/Houston) - development is a local business

## Summary

Phone call from a cold email. Developer C does large commercial projects (Florida base, also Houston). Permitting delays exist but on $100M+ projects they are managed by bigger teams and feel less "physical"; residential and smaller shops feel the pain most.

## Key points

- **Permitting challenge spectrum:** large commercial has more people and a more managed process. Smaller municipalities and residential are hit harder. Municipal politics matter (pro-development vs anti). Personnel matters: some jurisdictions are chronically understaffed, others outsource review to third parties that have an incentive to be thorough, even heavy-handed, to justify their fees.
- **Market entry:** assemble the right local Rolodex and local engineers and architects who know the market, e.g., people who have done 50 projects in that municipality. "Development is a local business, period." No substitute for market experience; the only path is diligence: keep pushing and adapt as surprises appear.
- **Code consultants:** he does not use them often in Houston (lighter regulatory environment) but recommends specialized code consultants for complex or regulated project types (hospitals, schools, data centers).
