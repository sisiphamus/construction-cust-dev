# SaaS Vendor Rep A (field-service software for trades) - pen-and-paper contractors and an all-in-one pitch

Note: the interviewer took the call as a prospective small-trade customer, so this shows how the vendor frames contractor pain.

## What the rep said about contractors

- Talks to many contractors, most of whom hardly make it past one year in business.
- Electricians: the number one thing he hears is that it is hard to manage everything; many still invoice on pen and paper because they have always done it that way.
- A typical electrician day is 8am to 8pm, then 2-3 hours of admin. Running everything on spreadsheets is "crazy"; the product is "even more simple than spreadsheets."
- Disorganization from paying for accounting software, a time clock, and other separate tools.

## What the product covers (pitch)

Central customer system, quotes, scheduling employees and jobs, customer communications, expense and labor-cost tracking, material expenses, invoicing, timely payment collection (avoid large receivables), payment processing, after-sales, marketing, Google reviews (emphasized), referral system, email campaigns, and follow-up on missed jobs. Subscription includes multi-session onboarding training and a product expert who shows how similar companies use it and tailors a plan.

## Growth claims

Two levers: bring in more customers or increase job size. Claimed ~37% average revenue increase. Referral idea: tell clients that referring someone gets 25% off the next order. Reviews are critical.

## Discovery questions the rep asked

Tracking win rate? Upselling beyond the original call? Quote on the spot or later? Who handles follow-up? How are calls taken? Is there a calendar? Missing jobs or showing up late? How are hours tracked (clock in/out)? How are card payments processed?
