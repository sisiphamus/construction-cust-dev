# Flooring Sub Owner A (small flooring business) - steady work, undercut commercial bids, and payment terms

## Tools
QuickBooks for estimates and invoices ("super easy"); quotes, invoices and receipts all through email. A missed call goes to voicemail.

## Pains
- **Consistency:** "staying consistent", having workflow all the time and income every week for his crews.
- **Low-bid pressure:** residential pays more but has many competitors; he matches or beats competing quotes by $400-500. On commercial properties prices get so low he cannot knock off $1,500 ("the math isn't adding up") and does not understand how others bid so low.
- **Payment terms:** residential pays as soon as the job is done. Commercial is often net 15 or net 30; a labor and materials price is submitted up front, and once approved the GC funds materials.
- Has not had an issue with any GC, though "some GCs are just jerks". Just landed a big commercial job.

## Business
- Residential jobs range from ~250 to 3,500 sq ft; he prefers larger ones but takes any. Residential charges for trash haul-off, floor removal, moving furniture and installation. Commercial "has the best money".
- Selling points: great online reviews, strong customer service, insured, 1-year guarantee on work, gives past-client phone numbers as references, will take calls or texts at any hour, and will negotiate if his bid is a bit high. Some customers chose him solely from reviews.
- Hiring: workers find him via Google and ask to join; new hires get a one-week trial then a raise to normal rate if they prove themselves.
