# Civil Engineer A (Texas college-town-area civil/land development) - 2-week review rounds even with no changes

## Process
1. Acquire raw land (a college-town area has zoning; Houston does not).
2. Plat (preliminary then final plat).
3. Site plan design: 2-3 months depending on size.
4. City permitting: 3-4 months typical.
5. Construction.

## City review cycle
Standard review 2 weeks; address comments 1-2 weeks; resubmit and wait another 2 weeks even if scope did not change. Typically 2-3 rounds, even for unchanged resubmittals. He does not change plans between some reviews but still waits two weeks each round.

## Design coordination
Site plan complexity rises as architects, MEP, structural and electrical join. Coordination is manual and fragmented. Early civil involvement reduces rework. Permitting timelines are predictable but inflexible.

## Opportunity angle
Comment management on resubmittals, submission-preparation verification, and design coordination tooling.
