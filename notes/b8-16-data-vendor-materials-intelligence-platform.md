# Data Vendor Rep A (construction project-data / materials-intelligence platform) - product tour at a national architecture conference

## Summary

The rep demonstrated a database of about 3.7M construction projects and a new Materials Intelligence product that lets building-product manufacturers search which projects use their products and where architects specify them.

## Key points

- **Coverage:** about 3.7M active projects: civil (airports, dams, bridges, sewers, water/wastewater) and general (commercial, community, condos, multifamily). Single-family housing is excluded: easy to pull from permits but messy, low-value contracts. Commercial is more stable with predictable revenue and lower default risk.
- **Content sourcing:** public data via public-records requests and web bots; private data via a content team of about 500 (about 1,200 including offshore) that manually calls contractors and developers daily. Relationships with contractors and architects are the moat, along with data access.
- **Material Intelligence:** OCR plus AI searches plans and specs by keyword (a single concrete-product keyword returned 55,000+ projects). Manufacturers see which architects specify competitors and approach those architects for the next project. Manufacturers want to be the "basis of design" rather than "or equal."
- **Analytics dashboard:** construction trends by state, county and metro area, multifamily vs single-family spend, and where growth is happening.
- **Customers and channel:** the sister company that sells design software to roughly 65,000 architects is the biggest customer and embeds the product-spec library. Building-product manufacturers (insulation, drywall and similar) are customers.
- **Sales structure:** business development reps prospect; sales reps run demo, negotiation and close, so prospecting and closing are separate workloads. One person handles collections, mostly net-30 to net-90 payment term conversations, not chasing small defaults.
- A code-database startup also regularly approaches them about their sales process and market engagement, and is interested in their data.

## Relevance

Queryable construction project database and material intelligence from specs; product-placement tracking for manufacturers. No buy signal; no ask.
