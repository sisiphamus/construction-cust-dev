# Logistics Executive A (Israeli freight forwarder) - AI to cut documentation labor

## Business
- Roughly 95% ocean import containers (mostly China), about 3% air, about 2% export; also strong in Turkey and parts of Europe. Includes in-house customs brokers across commodities and three branches in the country. About 20-25k containers a year; about 70 staff.
- Differentiators: knowledge of the China market, relationships with the best origin agents, volume leverage for good carrier contracts. Opening an order from China is "plug and play"; customer gets an update within about 10 minutes. Works with partner agents worldwide; origin-side cost depends on them.

## What holds up shipments
- Far-east port congestion: space is booked about two weeks ahead.
- Customs release: customers forget to give authorities required approvals.
- Last mile: trucker capacity is scarce (many drivers tied up by the regional conflict), fuel costs are high, and truckers sometimes cancel at the last minute for a better offer; delivery can take 4-5 days and the forwarder must apologize to the customer.
- Finding a good partner agent in each region.

## Top concern: employee cost
- Since covid employee costs rose about 50-60%. Pays salaries in local currency but is paid in dollars. Hard to find young hires; logistics seen as unattractive and staff leave after 1-2 years. US forwarders face the same wage inflation and have outsourced many roles to call centers.
- Response: AI automation. Documents (commercial invoices, shipper charges, certificates) are now about 90% AI-read and loaded into the ERP, with a person checking; a pilot puts customs shipments into the system with AI. ERP is tied to vessel ETAs so customers get automatic delay updates. Hiring criteria shifted from fast typists to people who can work with AI and technology. Wants to keep staff and make them better, lowering cost-to-revenue ratio. A person in the office now owns AI/tech.

## Systems
- One ERP (accounting, customs clearance, transmission) used daily by all employees. Only three or four ERPs exist locally because large global ERPs do not localize for customs; they behave like a monopoly. The product is basically unchanged in 20 years; he pushes it to add features and it is adding AI tools.

## Notes
- He avoids using general AI chat tools personally because he does not want one provider holding all his data, though he sees the cost and revenue upside.
- Relevance to construction: supply-chain lead-time risk; labor cost pressure; AI back-office automation. No buy signal.
