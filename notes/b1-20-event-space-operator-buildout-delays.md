# Tenant Operator A (small event space) - waiting on city approval for build-out

- The original partner built the old venue; for the new space they used the contractor the current landlord uses across its properties. The design was sketched and handed to the crew. Work was split between the old partner and the landlord's builders.
- The landlord paid for the build-out as part of getting into the lease. The building existed but was fully exposed on arrival with no electrical done; equipment was transferred from the old venue.
- Most frustrating: waiting for city approval on electrical and plumbing before anything else could proceed.
- They are scheduling events against the build-out, so "timing is super important, it ruins trust."
