# GC Owner A (small home and commercial GC) - oversaturated market, informal operations

- **Estimating:** estimates with notes on a tablet; uses SketchUp Pro; drawing is slow, so he only draws once payments are collected.
- **Market:** a "cesspool of hungry people bidding over and over"; oversaturated, "everyone is a contractor now"; most just want to hang drywall and paint, though some trades (HVAC, electrical) require much more.
- **Sub management:** project managers watch the subs. If someone is not up to par they are cut, and the contract allows not paying them, even if it makes the project run long.
- **Leads:** organic, via Instagram or Google.
- **Materials:** cannot control acts of god; e.g., a material order from a tornado-hit state is simply delayed.
- **Operating style:** "I probably don't do things the way they're supposed to be done... I pray"; growth depends on good, non-corrupt jobs coming in.
