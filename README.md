# Construction customer development

190 anonymized customer-development conversation notes, May–July 2026, with people in construction: developers, GCs, subs, architects, engineers, city permitting staff, and software vendors. Focus is Houston/Texas permitting and the cash-flow pains of subcontractors.

- People are replaced with role placeholders (e.g. "Developer A"); employers are described generically (size, type, region).
- Contact details, recording IDs, personal and family details, and interviewer private info are removed. Dates are month-level.
- Software products stay where they are the subject of the conversation.
- Start with [INDEX.md](INDEX.md); notes are in `notes/`.

Private repo. Originals are not included.
